Live test streams

This site gives free looping live HLS streams for tests of P2P Media Loader, a library that delivers video segments between viewers over WebRTC.

Streams

Big Buck Bunny

Live https://live.novage.workers.dev/bbb/live.m3u8

VOD https://live.novage.workers.dev/bbb/vod.m3u8

  • 1080p, 720p and 360p, H.264 and AAC in fMP4
  • 6 s segments with aligned key frames in all renditions
  • 60 s live window; the video loops every 10 min 30 s
  • CORS is open to all origins

How it works

A Cloudflare Worker makes the live playlists from a VOD stream. The live position comes from the clock, so all viewers see the same live edge, and no encoder runs.
